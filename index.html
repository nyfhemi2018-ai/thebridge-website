01

The missing layer

Markets became digital.Governance remained fragmented.

Identity sits in one system. Risk in another. Capital mandates live in documents. Conduct is often visible only after loss.

FIC brings these signals into a continuous governance layer, allowing participation to be understood before, monitored during and evidenced after every mandate.

The operating method

Identity is a beginning. Governance must continue.

FIC follows participation through a continuous cycle that establishes who is involved, what rules apply, how conduct evolves and what the evidence shows.

01

Identify

Know the participant

Verified identity, role, readiness and permission establish who is participating and on what basis.

02

Define

Set the mandate

Risk boundaries, governance plans, compensation and participation terms become explicit before capital is exposed.

03

Govern

Monitor continuously

Behaviour, exposure and adherence are evaluated throughout participation, not only at onboarding.

04

Evidence

Make outcomes auditable

Structured records support intervention, due diligence, accountability and institutional learning.
